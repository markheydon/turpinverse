Good service looks effortless because someone checked the lamps, the larder, and the exits before anyone asked for soup.

Our hospitality clients borrow the same discipline we use on the road: predictable handovers, named owners, and a short list of things that must never be improvised. Discretion is not secrecy for its own sake. It is how you keep a guest comfortable when the world outside is nosy.

If your operations manual only exists for inspectors, it will fail the people who actually stay the night.
