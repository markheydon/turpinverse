Every quarter someone arrives with a colour-coded route map and calls it transformation. At Turpin Enterprises we prefer fewer colours and more honest mileage.

Corridor strategy is not about drawing straighter lines between market towns. It is about knowing which relationships will still answer the door after dark, which inns will hold a horse without questions, and which shortcuts are folklore rather than fact.

We review corridor plans the same way we review ledgers: who benefits, who is exposed, and whether the story survives contact with a muddy Tuesday.
