Clients hire consultants because they want confidence. We sell something more useful: a framework for deciding before the weather turns.

Uncertainty is not a branding problem. It is the operating environment. The executive who claims to have eliminated risk has usually just moved it into a footnote someone else will read at assize.

Our leadership practice starts with naming what we do not know, then sequencing decisions so the team can act before the map goes out of date.
