Horses are not motorcycles with opinions, though many project plans assume otherwise.

Myth one: speed is always the constraint. Myth two: any stable is interchangeable. Myth three: the animal's temperament is a branding problem. Logistics fails at the junction of all three.

We publish this for the transport team, the finance team, and anyone who has ever said "just leave at dawn" without meeting the ostler.
