The Black Bess Route Optimiser began as a joke on a whiteboard and became the project that forced us to agree on definitions.

Pressure routing is not about finding the heroic shortcut. It is about preserving options when the horse is tired, the light is failing, and the client still expects a confident ETA. We built the optimiser around recoverability metrics, not vanity miles saved.

If you only read one article about how Turpin Enterprises thinks about transport tooling, make it this one.
