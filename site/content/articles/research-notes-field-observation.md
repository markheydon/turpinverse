Field research is not a treasure hunt. It is disciplined noticing: who speaks first, what never gets written down, and which rituals everyone performs but nobody names.

Our research practice borrows from legend work and ledger work in equal measure. The story people tell is data. The invoice they avoid is also data.

These notes are for analysts who are tired of decks that confuse anecdote with pattern.
