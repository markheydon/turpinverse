# ADR-0010: Navigation grouping

## Status

**Accepted**

## Context

Hugo and Blazor must share information architecture.

## Decision

Directory, CRM, and Finance groups aligned across Hugo dropdowns and Blazor sidebar.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Parity between showcase and export app

### Negative

- Hugo has extra Universe group

## Alternatives Considered

Rejected scattering finance items as top-level Hugo entries.
